About Me

I'm Jie Cai (蔡杰), a Postdoctoral Researcher and Shuimu Tsinghua Scholar at the Pervasive HCI Group in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, where I work with Dr. Chun Yu. Before joining Tsinghua, I was an Assistant Research Professor at the College of Information Sciences and Technology, Penn State University, where I co-led the Collaboration and Innovation Lab with Dr. John M. Carroll. I received my Ph.D. from the Ying Wu College of Computing at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, where I was advised by Dr. Yvette Wohn and worked at the Social X Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, Computer-Supported Cooperative Work, and human-AI collaboration. I study how AI systems reshape social interaction, responsibility, and collaboration in everyday settings. My current work focuses on family-centered AI and AI-supported education, including parent–AI collaboration, parent-child communication, children’s learning and emotional development, and the design of parent-centered AI systems. A central goal of my research is to understand how AI can support, rather than replace, human care, instruction, and judgment.

This work builds on a broader agenda in platform governance, online communities, and human-AI collaboration. Across these areas, I examine how people make sense of technological systems, negotiate roles and responsibilities, and manage risks when digital technologies become embedded in social life. I use mixed methods, including interviews, observation, surveys, participatory design, system building, and field evaluation.

My work has been published in premier HCI venues, including CHI and CSCW, and has received four Paper Awards. I actively serve the HCI community through organizing roles such as ACM CHI 2027 Proceedings Co-Chair, ICHEC 2026 Paper Co-Chair,ACM CSCW 2024, 2025, and 2026 Publication Co-Chair, ACM CHI 2022 Assistant to the Technical Program Chairs, and ACM IMX 2021 Proceedings Co-Chair. I also serve on program committees for CSCW, CHI, and CHI PLAY, and have organized workshops at CHI 2023 and CHI 2024.
